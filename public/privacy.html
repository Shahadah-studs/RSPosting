RedStonePosting Privacy

🔒 Privacy Policy

1. DATA MINIMIZATION We only log what is absolutely essential to hold your feed together: your custom username, secure password configuration, and the flat text strings you push to the public message board.
2. SECURE INFO STORAGE Our main database architecture tokens and project links are smacked directly inside Vercel’s private environment variable vaults. Your account records are isolated, meaning public source pushes on GitHub can never view your credentials.
3. CREDENTIAL AUTOMATION Our login/sign up text inputs are built with standard auto-fill parameters so Google Password Manager works seamlessly. We do not track your browsing habits; we just make sure your browser can store your keys safely so you don't lose them.
4. MULTIMEDIA DISPATCHES When you share screenshot link structures for tournament voting, the pictures load via external hosts like Discord or Imgur. RedStonePosting stores zero tracking data for these media items, preserving database metrics.
5. ENCRYPTED CHANNELS All community outreach logs, rank inquiries, and verification submissions sent to our platform inbox are safely handled by the secure end-to-end encrypted tunnels of ProtonMail. The platform is operated under the developer identity of Shahadah_Studios.
